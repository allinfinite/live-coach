# Live Coach announcement draft

Live Coach helps you stay clear in difficult conversations. It offers recent observations, a short response to consider, and quick actions for suggesting a reply, examining a pattern, or ending gracefully.

Built for macOS, it combines local speech recognition with Ollama coaching. Capture starts only when you choose Start and acknowledge that participants have been informed as appropriate. Call recordings are not saved; transcript retention is optional. You can enroll your own voice with consent to read suggestions aloud locally.

This is an early open-source project from DNA Levity, derived from Meetily Community Edition under the MIT license. Thanks to the Meetily contributors for the audio and recognition foundation. Live Coach is independently maintained and is not an official Meetily product.

The first focus is personal coaching: boundaries, clarity, and difficult conversations. Broader sales, assistant and project-tool workflows are being explored. The current release does not autonomously carry on calls or control your computer.

Try the source build, share a synthetic reproduction of a problem, or contribute an improvement. Treat suggestions as options you can accept, change or ignore.
