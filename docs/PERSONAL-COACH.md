# Use Live Coach for a personal conversation

Start with a low-stakes conversation so you can judge whether the suggestions help. Wear headphones if you use Read aloud.

## Prepare

Choose Personal coach in Conversation settings. Set a concrete goal, such as “Agree on a fair division of this week's chores.” Set your intention, such as “Be direct, listen, and avoid explaining my boundary over and over.” Add only the relationship context that is useful for this call.

Ollama should show ready and a local speech model should be selected. Opening the app warms the local coach without starting capture. Inform participants as appropriate, acknowledge capture, and press Start conversation yourself.

## During the conversation

Use Quiet mode when you want to request help only at a pause. Suggest reply offers a short sentence; Analyze pattern looks at recent behavior; End gracefully offers a way to close or pause. Treat an observation as a hypothesis to check against what happened. You decide what to say.

For example, if a request keeps returning after you have answered, a useful goal is to state your answer clearly and decide whether more discussion is useful. A constructive reply might be: “I understand that you want more detail; my answer is still no, and I would like us to discuss another option.” A suggestion should not claim to know the other person's motive.

Read aloud uses your saved local voice sample. Transcription pauses while it plays, so choose a natural break. It plays through your Mac's output and does not speak into the other person's call.

## Finish

Press Stop. By default, the transcript disappears. If you opted into retention, Delete now removes the transcript and the retention period controls later expiry. Your separately enrolled voice sample remains until Forget voice.

The app coaches you during conversations. For project tasks, open the separate Codex projects section, select context and review the task before sending it. See [Codex integration](CODEX-INTEGRATION.md). Autonomous conversation participation remains future work.
